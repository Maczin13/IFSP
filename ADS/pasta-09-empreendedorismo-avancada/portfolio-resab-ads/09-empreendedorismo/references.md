# Referências anotadas

- Ries, Eric. *The Lean Startup*. Crown Business, 2011 — MVP, ciclo construir-medir-aprender e aprendizagem validada.
- Croll, Alistair; Yoskovitz, Benjamin. *Lean Analytics*. O'Reilly, 2013 — métricas de tração, uma métrica que importa e coortes.
- Intercom. “RICE scoring model”: https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/ — Reach, Impact, Confidence e Effort.
- Kniberg, Henrik; Skarin, Mattias. *Kanban and Scrum, making the most of both*: https://www.infoq.com/minibooks/kanban-scrum-minibook/ — fluxo, WIP e entrega incremental.
- Osterwalder, Alexander; Pigneur, Yves. *Business Model Generation*. Wiley, 2010 — estrutura do Business Model Canvas.
